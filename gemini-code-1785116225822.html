Welcome to Spring Valley

Challenging Golf Set in Natural Foothills

Spring Valley Golf Course offers a challenging 18-hole layout measuring 6,140 yards from the tips across 4 tee locations. Set in the beautiful Milpitas foothills away from housing developments, players are tested with picturesque Par 3s, doglegs, difficult Par 4s, and reachable Par 5s with water and sand hazards.
